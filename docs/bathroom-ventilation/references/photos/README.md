## Implications for the enclosure proposal

Measure the distance from the original wood ceiling to the fan's grille mounting plane and to the lowest part of the duct/elbow. For a compact-cover layout, size the cover around the fan, connections, supports, finish and service access. Show the exposed duct and elbow projection separately; they may extend lower than the cover. The visible duct appearance and performance both need a measured design.

For an elongated enclosure, a matching second grille could conceal access beneath the duct section. The image shows that section on the fan's discharge side: a spare grille opening into the surrounding enclosure would not, by itself, connect that cavity to the fan's intake. Passive exchange and deliberate exhaust airflow remain different design cases.

This is a visual assessment, not a measured installation drawing or confirmation of hidden conditions, support, duct sealing, airflow, electrical work or roof weatherproofing. The earlier elongated-enclosure estimates do not apply to a compact cover.
