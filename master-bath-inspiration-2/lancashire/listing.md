## Why this listing matters for our remodel

The listing description names the exact team and products used — this is the most actionable part:

- **Kitchen and bathrooms designed by Destination Eichler.** That is the same Destination Eichler already in our sources (they collaborate with Fireclay Tile on an Eichler-specific collection). This house is effectively a built example of that collaboration.
- **Fireclay Tile** throughout the baths — consistent with the tile research in `../master-bath-inspiration-2/README.md`.
- **Custom Sequoia bamboo cabinetry** — the warm wood vanities in the photos are bamboo, not walnut. Worth knowing: bamboo achieves that warm mid-century slab-front look and is a common Eichler-appropriate alternative.
- **Hansgrohe fixtures.**
- **Period-correct VCT flooring** — the gray speckled floor in the bathroom photos is vinyl composition tile, *not* terrazzo. It's a cheap, authentic way to get a speckled mid-century floor. Useful counterpoint to the terrazzo pricing in `../master-bath-inspiration-2/shower-pans-terrazzo.md`, though VCT is not appropriate inside a wet shower pan.
- Other signature elements: open-air atrium, walls of glass, mahogany paneling, tongue-and-groove ceilings.
- Infrastructure: owned solar (net-zero annual electricity), electrical and roof improvements, sewer lateral repairs, drainage. Reportedly ~$500k of improvements over 13 years.

## Where the bamboo / Fireclay / VCT details came from — and how far to trust them

All three come from **the listing page's own text**, read directly off the page, not inferred from the photos:

- **Bamboo cabinetry, Fireclay Tile, Hansgrohe, Destination Eichler** — stated in the MLS public remarks (first excerpt above). This is the listing agent's own description of the finishes.
- **VCT flooring** — stated in the remarks (second excerpt) and corroborated independently by the structured Property Details table, which lists **Flooring: Vinyl**. Two separate fields on the page agree.

Confidence and caveats, honestly:

- MLS remarks are **agent-written marketing copy**, not a verified spec sheet. They're usually accurate about named brands (agents get these from the owner), but they are a claim, not proof.
- The remarks describe **the house as a whole**. They do not say photo-by-photo which room has which finish, so "the bathroom floor is VCT" is a reasonable read of "period-correct VCT flooring" + "Flooring: Vinyl" — but it is not separately confirmed for that specific room. The visual (uniform gray field with fine multi-color speckle, no aggregate depth, no grout lines) is consistent with VCT.
- **"Sequoia"** is most likely the cabinet maker/brand rather than a species; the material stated is bamboo.
- My initial read of the photos called the vanities "walnut" — that was **wrong**, corrected by the listing text. Warm bamboo with a vertical grain match reads very much like walnut in photos, which is itself a useful finding: bamboo is a cheaper, Eichler-appropriate way to get that look.
- Worth verifying at a showing or by asking the listing agent (Theresa Lee, Coldwell Banker) if any of this drives a purchasing decision.
